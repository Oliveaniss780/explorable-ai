_A running list of the tools I reach for. Inspired by [uses.tech](https://uses.tech)._

## Making the explainers

- **Visualizations:** hand-written SVG and `<canvas>`, driven by the site's theme variables so every widget flips with light/dark mode.
- **This site:** a **custom static-site generator** that turns Markdown into a linked digital garden. The interactive widgets are mounted from plain `<div>` placeholders dropped into Markdown.

## Writing & thinking

- **Notes:** a plain-text, Markdown-first vault, published straight to the web.
- **Diagrams:** sketched by hand first, then rebuilt as code.

## Design language

- **Type:** DM Serif Display (headings), Bricolage Grotesque (body), JetBrains Mono (code).
- **Palette:** a two-ink letterpress scheme, deep navy + vermilion on warm paper, inverted to a cyanotype at night.
