Stuck on a term? Curious how something works? Ask below. The answer is written by an AI model that runs **entirely in your browser** — no login, no key, and nothing you type leaves your device.

<div class="explorable" data-explorable="ask-ai"></div>

Your first question downloads the model once (a few hundred megabytes); after that it works offline and answers quickly. It runs on WebGPU, so use Chrome or Edge on a computer for the smoothest experience. For search that points you to the exact notes in this garden, try **ask the garden** on the [home page](/).

Answers are generated by a small local model, so they can be imperfect — treat replies as a helpful starting point, then follow the garden's links for the careful version.
